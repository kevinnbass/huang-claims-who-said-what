Who said it first, and who followed

Each bar is the sources (an outlet, or a person or body in its own statement) whose earliest statement of the claim picked, flat or hedged, falls on that day among the documents read; the darker part is those that stated it flatly that first day. A document's day is the day it was first published, where its page shows one. Select a bar to read each source's first statement: they are listed in order of the time of publication where the page carries one (shown in UTC), and after those the pages that give only a day.

What it rests on

Only statements that state the claim, flatly or with a hedge. The middle column is who says it; the right column is the evidence the sentence, or the attribution governing it, names as its basis. It is not a finding about what the outlet holds. Select a block or a band to read the statements.

Who credits whom

Statements on the claim picked that are put to another outlet's reporting. Rows are the outlet writing, columns the outlet it credits, as named in the sentence. An outlet in its own column is crediting its own earlier report. Select a number to read the statements.
